About this simulation

Known: left eye best-known acuity 20/70; right eye no vision. With both eyes open, her effective vision is her left eye alone. Correction status is unknown.

Simulated: uniform Gaussian optical blur, in linear light. Acuity is established; converting it to Gaussian blur is an engineering approximation. σ spans 1.5–2.4 arcmin: roughly MAR-sized FWHM to MTF ≈ 0.1 at 8.6 cycles/degree. No acuity result uniquely specifies a blur kernel.

Not simulated: missing or unnoticed objects, glare, blind spots, tunnel vision, darkening, color shifts, or a black region for her right eye. Optional contrast reduction starts off.

Depth: loss of stereo depth perception means reliance on monocular cues; fine depth tasks such as stairs, curbs and pouring can be harder. A flat screen cannot show this.

Unknown: cause of 20/70, correction status, contrast sensitivity, glare sensitivity, actual visual field and performance in dim light.

Reduced awareness of objects in view is not explained by 20/70 acuity alone; possible causes need clinical evaluation (contrast sensitivity, field testing, cognition).

Limits

A camera is not an eye. Camera focus, sharpening, compression, exposure and lens FOV affect the result. Small screens shrink the scene and its blur. The filter keeps the scene-true blur; it does not amplify it to compensate for the viewer. Wide-angle perspective makes pixels per degree a frame-average approximation.

The reading categories use a 2× acuity reserve as an engineering estimate. Fluent reading is not guaranteed; a person’s critical print size can be larger. The synthetic chart is not a certified optotype test or a diagnosis.

Intermediate blur uses half-float linear buffers when supported, with a disclosed 8-bit linear fallback. The Gaussian radius is capped at 48 pixels; any truncation or image resizing is reported. Thirty frames per second is a target, not a guarantee. In slow camera sessions, processing resolution may reduce, with its new pixel scale reported.

The PNG caption records the blur and optional contrast state. Exported snapshots exclude the hold-to-compare original. This filter does not replace an eye exam.

Privacy

No libraries, analytics, fonts, uploads, remote image calls or network APIs. Media stays in this page’s memory and is discarded on reload. Only the blur setting and numeric task presets are saved on this device. Save Snapshot creates a local file. GitHub serves the page itself; it does not receive camera or photo content.
