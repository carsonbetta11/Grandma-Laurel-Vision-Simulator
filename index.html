About this approximation

Left eye: best-known acuity 20/70. Right eye: no vision. The photo has uniform blur; no part is masked to represent the blind eye.

The blur is fixed to the full photo before zooming. Enlarging it lets you inspect the same modeled view. It does not represent moving closer to the photographed object.

20/70 corresponds to a 3.5-arcmin minimum angle of resolution and a 17.5-arcmin letter height. [ESTABLISHED] acuity conversion Gaussian blur is an engineering approximation, not a measurement of her exact visual experience.

Cause, correction status, contrast sensitivity, glare sensitivity and actual visual field are unknown. Stereo depth loss, field loss, missing objects and reduced awareness are not simulated. Reduced awareness of objects in view is not explained by 20/70 acuity alone; possible causes need clinical evaluation.

Display size, viewing distance, source focus and image quality affect the impression. This is a rough family demonstration, not a medical assessment.

Blur is computed in linear light. Large images are reduced to a maximum 4096-pixel side or the device’s smaller limit, with the scale adjusted. A 48-pixel kernel cap and an 8-bit linear-buffer fallback are disclosed if used.

Photos stay in this page’s memory and disappear on reload. Only your blur setting is saved locally. No uploads, analytics, accounts, libraries, external assets or live camera access.
